### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Management Information Systems (MIS)** graduate software developer and business analyst who bridges the gap between complex engineering and strategic business operations. Combining years of commercial management experience with full-stack software development skills, I design scalable ERP/CRM architectures, robust web platforms, and AI-driven simulation systems.

- 🚀 **Founder & Lead Developer** at **Sports Simulation Studios**, developing AI-driven 3D referee training simulations using Unity & C#.
- 🏗️ **Enterprise Architect**, engineering full-lifecycle ERP, CRM, and financial accounting platforms with Next.js, .NET, React, and Supabase/PostgreSQL.
- 🤖 **AI & Modern Integrations Enthusiast**, implementing Generative AI (GPT-4o Vision & OCR), Model Context Protocol (MCP), and automated background workflows.
- 🎯 **Methodologies**: Agile / Scrum, SDLC, Systems Analysis & Requirements Engineering (Business Analysis), CRISP-DM.
